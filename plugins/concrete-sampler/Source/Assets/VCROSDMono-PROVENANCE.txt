text. The font's own internal `name` table has no copyright, license description, license URL, or
trademark fields at all (checked with fontTools) - the only relevant field is Designer (nameID 9),
"MrManet", which does not match "Riciery Leal", the designer widely credited across download-
aggregator sites. No verified license text could be found for this exact file's redistribution
rights in a commercial, distributed binary. Shipped anyway per an explicit, informed decision by
Adam Beck (Wild Jag Audio) made 2026-09-10 after this ambiguity was raised - see
plugins/concrete-sampler/ui-plan.md's "Open items to resolve during the mockup" (VCR OSD Mono
licensing) for the original flag, and this plugin's Phase 8 JUCE build-out session for the
back-and-forth. Revisit if this is ever challenged.
