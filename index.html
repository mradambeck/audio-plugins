macOS only. Each installer lets you choose which formats to install (AU / VST3 / Standalone) and whether to install for all users of the Mac or just yourself.

Unsigned. These builds are ad-hoc signed, not notarized with a paid Apple Developer ID. On first run, Gatekeeper may block the installer — right-click it and choose Open, or approve it under System Settings → Privacy & Security.

"Just the plugin" downloads are the raw .component/.vst3 file, zipped — unzip it and drag it into ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components (AU) or ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3 (VST3) yourself. Since there's no installer to carry the Gatekeeper approval, macOS may refuse to load it in your DAW until you clear the quarantine flag it picked up from the browser download: xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/Name.component (swap in the actual path). The regular installer above doesn't need this.
