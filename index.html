Free & open source

Six hardware-inspired audio plugins for macOS

Bucket-brigade delay, fuzz, overdrive, phaser, a stacked mono synth, and a pitch-shifting delay — each one built with JUCE, styled after 1980s rack-gear hardware, and free to use under the AGPLv3.

Install everything at once

One .pkg with a checkbox per plugin and per format (AU / VST3 / Standalone).

Download All Six

Caverns

Delay

A bucket-brigade-style stereo delay with independent L/R time, tape-style degrade and modulation for pitch wobble and lo-fi character, and tempo sync.

AU · VST3 · Standalone Download

Damage

Fuzz

FM-mangled fuzz — a square-wave/pulse-width fuzz stage that can be cross-modulated by its own internal oscillator, plus a level-following noise gate.

AU · VST3 · Standalone Download

Corrosion

Overdrive

Tanh-based overdrive/distortion with an asymmetric bias stage for even-harmonic warmth and an optional rectifier stage for a more aggressive character.

AU · VST3 · Standalone Download

Flux

Phaser

An analog-style phase shifter with a switchable stage count from 2 to 36, plus a Color section for shaping the phased signal before the dry/wet blend.

AU · VST3 · Standalone Download

Alloy

Synth

A stacked mono synth combining an analog oscillator with unison and a sub, an FM voice, and a built-in arpeggiator — voiced for bass, usable for anything.

AU · VST3 · Standalone Download

Gradient

Pitch Delay

A pitch-shifting delay with two independent units that can run in Dual mode, Linked, or Cross-feedback, for evolving, self-modulating textures.

AU · VST3 · Standalone Download
macOS only. Each installer lets you choose which formats to install (AU / VST3 / Standalone) and whether to install for all users of the Mac or just yourself.

Unsigned. These builds are ad-hoc signed, not notarized with a paid Apple Developer ID. On first run, Gatekeeper may block the installer — right-click it and choose Open, or approve it under System Settings → Privacy & Security.
